# Blink Hunters

A sky time-lapse for NASA's SPHEREx infrared survey (NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026, "PlanetX and SphereX").
Type a star name or RA/Dec to blink the same patch of sky across SPHEREx's six-monthly visits, see the
difference or a colour composite, add and subtract any visits, and click any object for its 102-band
spectrum. The five demo targets are pre-built into this Space; other targets are fetched live from
NASA/IPAC IRSA and the SPHEREx copy on AWS.

Share a view: `https://<this space>.hf.space/#q=Barnard%27s%20Star&det=6&mode=diff`

SPHEREx data: NASA/IPAC Infrared Science Archive (IRSA), doi:10.26131/IRSA678.
